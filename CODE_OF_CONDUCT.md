# Code of conduct

PeriHub's issues, discussions and pull requests are for people who want their mice and keyboards to
work, and for people who help them. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience, background or
the device they own.

**Expected:** being patient and kind, especially with people new to GitHub or to testing; assuming
good intent; keeping to the topic; giving the details that help others help you.

**Not accepted:** insults, harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or
images, sharing someone's private information, and spam or advertising.

**What happens:** the maintainer may edit or hide comments, lock threads, and block people who do
not follow these rules, after a warning when it makes sense. To report something, use GitHub's
**Report content** on the comment, or open a private report from the repository's **Security** tab
if it should not be public.
